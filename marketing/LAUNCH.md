## Before you post

1. **UptimeRobot must be running** (see `docs/SERVICES.md`). Otherwise the first visitors from a post wait 30 to 50 seconds and leave.
2. Open the game yourself on a phone and a computer. Play one night to warm everything up.
3. Post in the US evening (about 6 to 9 pm Eastern). Stay around for the first hour to answer comments. Early replies push a post up.
4. Each subreddit has its own self-promotion rules. Read the sidebar before posting, and use the flair they ask for.

## After launch: what to watch

Open the stats page daily (`/admin/stats?token=...`, see `docs/SERVICES.md`):

- **Funnel:** visited, joined a room, started a night, escaped. The biggest drop is the next thing to fix.
- **Tutorial:** which tip people stop at.
- **Deaths by minute:** lots of deaths in the first minute means Night 1 is too hard for newcomers.
- **Intro skipped vs watched:** if most skip in the first 2 seconds, shorten it.
